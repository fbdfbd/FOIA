4. outputRules.csv
------------------
General rule principle:
- More specific survival/success rules get higher priority.
- Fallback danger rules get lower priority.
- Required history is a subsequence match.
- "No flashlight in pit" is represented by:
  high priority flashlight pit survival rule first,
  lower priority generic pit missing rule second.

Recommended priority bands:
- 1000+: final/scan-specific rules
- 950: final success route
- 900-940: tool-specific success/failure exploration
- 800: record/death setup routes
- 700: early/default routes and fallbacks
